# The Village

The page behind an NFC ornament: a winter mountain village that grows a little every day from December 11 through January 1.

- Live at https://tapdapine.online
- Everything is in `index.html`, with no build step and no dependencies.
- The season start date is `CFG` at the top of the script. The story is the `STORY` list just below it.
- Preview any day locally by opening `index.html` (a preview bar appears), or add `?preview` to the live URL.
